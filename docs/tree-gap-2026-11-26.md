# Tree gap: why rustfish needs ~2.1x Clover's nodes to depth 12 (2026-11-26)

Question (owner): rustfish needs 2.1x Clover 9.1's nodes (and 2.2x its time)
to reach depth 12. Why, concretely, measured?

Rules kept: Clover is a black box (its binary built from its release tag by
the existing peer-match job; only its UCI output was read: depth, seldepth,
nodes, time, PV move). Everything else is our own instrumentation on branch
`diag-tree-gap` (not for merge). Nothing was queued.

## Short answer

Measured on 350 positions against Clover's own UCI output and with our own
counters and one-knob counterfactuals. At depth 12 the counted-node ratio is
**2.44x** (time 2.4x). It breaks down into three multiplicative parts:

1. **x1.26: we count nodes differently.** Every frontier leaf counts
   twice, once as a depth-0 `negamax` entry and once in `quiescence`
   (20.9% of our counted nodes at depth 12). Clover's tiny searches count
   one node per position (black box: 9 vs our 12 nodes at depth 1 with 6
   legal moves). In equal units the depth-12 tree ratio is **1.93x**, and
   per position visited we are ~20-25% slower than Clover, not at parity.
   This also biases the 200k-node Clover gauge against us by ~20% of
   effort.
2. **x1.53 (x1.75 at depth 16): the singular extension.** It fires on
   ~45% of eligible nodes (39% of expected-CUT tries) and is the only
   mechanism that moves the tree by more than ~20%. It is the part of
   the gap that **grows with depth** and the whole of our seldepth excess
   over Clover. **But it pays for itself:** without it we lose **-47 Elo at
   fixed nodes and -28 at 10+0.1**; extending only clear singulars
   (verification < singular beta - 30) still loses -17. These nodes are
   not waste. A wider margin (4*depth: -25% nodes at depth 16) is about
   even at fixed nodes (-5.7 +- 13) and is an LTC candidate.
3. **x1.27 (x1.41 at depth 16) the rest:** less reduction at expected-ALL
   nodes (x1.11 at depth 12, x1.23 at 16, x1.29 at 20; reducing one ply
   more there scores **+10.1 [-3, +23] at fixed nodes**), the check
   extension (x1.07 with SE off; removing it is neutral, +1.8 +- 13), and
   ~x1.05-1.07 nothing single explains (aspiration, LMP, RFP, futility,
   NMP, ProbCut, qsearch, capture LMR, history pruning each move
   nodes by 5% or less).

So "2.1x" is not one broken mechanism. A quarter of it is bookkeeping, the
biggest real part is an extension that is worth its cost, and the
remainder is a modestly less aggressive reduction policy at ALL nodes
(plus a little check extension). Two LTC candidates are pushed:
`tree-lmr-all-node` and `tree-se-margin-4`.

## Method

- **Positions:** `scripts/diag/treegap/positions.txt` = the 300-position
  diag-mid300 set (game positions from the Clover/anchor games) + the 50
  OpenBench FENs = 350 positions; by phase 252 opening/early middlegame, 68
  middlegame, 24 late middlegame, 6 endgame (non-pawn material).
- **Runs:** `scripts/diag/treegap/run.py` (stdlib) plays one engine through
  the positions with `ucinewgame` + `go depth D` or `go movetime M`, Hash 16,
  1 thread, and keeps every completed iteration. `ci.sh` plans run on GitHub
  runners through a `tree:` mode of `peer-match.yml` on this branch
  (one search at a time for anything timed; node counts are deterministic and
  were reproduced exactly on a repeat run and on the desktop).
- **Our counters:** cargo feature `treestats` (`src/treediag.rs`): nodes by
  depth remaining and expected node type, exit reason, TT hit/cut, NMP,
  LMR/PVS re-searches, extensions, inclusive subtree nodes per mechanism,
  nodes by ply, seldepth.
- **Counterfactuals:** UCI option `DiagKnobs` (always compiled on this
  branch; defaults reproduce main, `Bench: 216602` unchanged) switches one
  mechanism at a time. Quality of a knob is judged by **games at fixed nodes**
  (200k nodes per move, 800 games, same binary with and without the knob,
  `peer-match.yml` with `scripts/diag/treegap/peers.json`) and at 10+0.1.
- Runs: black box 45812088326 (depth 20 x2 + movetime 1000), counting
  convention 45812486413, knobs depth 16: 45813190853, 45814494194; depth
  12/20: 45815285528 / 45815281013; games: sex_0 45814498435 (200k nodes) and 45814502824 (10+0.1); sxm_30 45816374457; sem_4 45816379745; ce_0 45816956262; allr_1 45816951340. sxm sweep and the singular-gap histogram on the desktop (`~/agent-runs/tree21`, 2 threads, nice 19). Diagnosis build = main 59680fb + knobs; main has since gained f62fa35 / 08a11ce (fail-soft futility bound, root static eval), not included.

## 1. Black box: the gap grows with depth

Sum over 350 positions, `go depth 20` (same runner, one search at a time):

| depth | rustfish nodes | Clover nodes | ratio | rustfish EBF | Clover EBF | rf time s | Clover time s | rf seldepth* | Clover seldepth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | 1.64M | 0.54M | 3.06 | 1.68 | 2.53 | 0.8 | 0.5 | - | 4.3 |
| 8 | 4.24M | 2.12M | 2.00 | 1.67 | 2.04 | 2.1 | 1.4 | 14.7 | 8.2 |
| 10 | 13.7M | 6.46M | 2.11 | 1.78 | 1.74 | 7.1 | 3.7 | 19.7 | 13.2 |
| 12 | 38.7M | 15.9M | **2.44** | 1.67 | 1.57 | 20.7 | 8.6 | 23.6 | 17.9 |
| 14 | 100M | 35.4M | 2.82 | 1.55 | 1.49 | 54.6 | 18.9 | 27.1 | 22.1 |
| 16 | 233M | 76.1M | 3.06 | 1.49 | 1.46 | 129 | 40.3 | 30.3 | 25.8 |
| 18 | 501M | 146M | 3.42 | 1.47 | 1.39 | 284 | 77.7 | - | 29.3 |
| 20 | 1026M | 270M | **3.80** | 1.40 | 1.35 | 589 | 143 | - | 32.8 |

(*) our seldepth from the treestats build (max ply incl. qsearch); Clover's
own convention reports seldepth 0 at depth 1, so its numbers read about one
ply lower than ours would.

- Counted NPS is at parity on this runner (1.87M vs 1.84M at depth 12), so
  time-to-depth tracks the node ratio: 2.4x at depth 12, 4.1x at depth 20.
- **The ratio is not constant:** 2.0x at depth 8-10, then it grows by ~5-6%
  per ply (our EBF is 0.05-0.10 above Clover's at every depth from 10 on).
  The 2.1x of the 25 Nov run (50 FENs) is the same curve at depth 12.
- By phase (depth 12, ratio of sums): opening/early middlegame 2.53,
  middlegame 2.22, late middlegame 2.15, endgame 1.38. Per position the
  ratio ranges 1.3x (p10) to 4.8x (p90). The gap is broad, largest where
  there are many pieces.
- **Depth semantics are comparable:** the share of positions whose depth-d
  move equals the engine's own depth-20 move climbs the same way for both
  (d12: rustfish 0.686, Clover 0.694; d16: 0.797 vs 0.769), and each
  engine's depth-d move agrees with the *other* engine's depth-20 move about
  equally (d16: 0.680 vs 0.669). A Clover ply is not a "smaller" ply.
- **Equal time (movetime 1000):** Clover reaches depth 23.8 vs our 18.9 with
  similar node counts (1.63M vs 1.46M), and changes its best move more often
  (2.82 changes from depth 5 on vs 1.68; 293 vs 233 positions with a change).
  Same final move in 242/350.

## 2. Part of "2.1x nodes" is how we count

Black box, tiny searches (`tree:cnt`): in K+P v K+P with 6 legal moves and
no captures anywhere, `go depth 1` reports **12 nodes for rustfish and 9 for
Clover**; startpos depth 1: 42 vs 25. rustfish counts each frontier leaf
twice: once in `negamax` (depth 0, which only runs the draw checks and calls
qsearch) and once in `quiescence`. Clover's counts are consistent with one
node per position (root + children + a few).

Our treestats: negamax-to-qsearch hand-offs are **22.4% / 20.9% / 19.9% /
19.2%** of all counted nodes at depth 10 / 12 / 14 / 16. In Clover's
convention our depth-12 count would be 38.7M x 0.79 = 30.6M: ratio **1.93x,
not 2.44x** (depth 16: 2.47x, not 3.06x).

Consequences:
- The "NPS parity" of 25 Nov is in counted nodes. Per position visited we
  are ~20-25% slower than Clover (consistent with the desktop's 1.6x NPS
  read, which also includes the build/CPU difference).
- **The 200k-node Clover gauge is biased against us:** at `nodes=200000`
  rustfish visits ~160k positions to Clover's 200k (-20% effort, worth very
  roughly 15-25 Elo at this node count). Read the -178 gauge as ~-155..-160
  in equal-work terms; the 10+0.1 result (-166) is unaffected.
- Not a strength lever by itself (the extra count is cheap), but every
  nodes-based comparison with peers should correct for it, or the count
  should move (proposal P5).

## 3. Where our nodes go (treestats, depth 16, 350 positions, 233M nodes)

- qsearch 37.4% of counted nodes (incl. the double-counted entry; qsearch
  nodes per entry 1.95, stand-pat cutoff 49.9%); negamax depth >= 1 46.0%;
  the rest are the depth-0 negamax entries.
- Negamax nodes by depth remaining: d1 55%, d2 22%, d3 9%, d4 5.5%,
  d5+ 8.5%. Expected CUT 76%, ALL 23.5%, PV 0.5%.
- Exits: CUT nodes leave by RFP 65%, TT 4.7%, NMP 6.0%, ProbCut 0.9%,
  move-loop fail high 17.7%, fail low 3.2%. ALL nodes: razor 18.8%, RFP
  25.4%, fail high 21.5%, fail low 27.2%.
- First-move cutoff 81.9% of move-loop fail highs (unchanged from earlier
  audits). Moves searched at ALL nodes d10+: 21.
- Inclusive subtree shares: NMP searches 18.6%, singular verification
  8.7%, ProbCut 4.9%, LMR re-searches 16.4%, aspiration-failed root
  searches 12.7% (864 fails in 6464 root searches), subtrees under checking
  moves 45.9%.
- **Singular extensions:** tried 374k times (CUT 313k, PV 38k, ALL 24k);
  the TT move is found singular in **39% of CUT, 57% of PV and 55% of ALL
  tries** (45% at depth 8, 40% at depth 12): every second eligible node
  extends its TT move by a ply.

| no IIR | 1.23 | 1.57 | 1.86 | 2.42 |

- **The singular extension is the only single mechanism that moves the
  tree by more than ~15%, and it is the one that grows with depth** (x1.0
  at depth 8, x1.32 at 10, x1.53 at 12, x1.75 at 16). It explains the
  growing part of the gap: with `sex_0` the rustfish/Clover ratio becomes
  flat across depth (counted nodes: 1.98 / 1.60 / 1.60 / 1.75 at depth
  8 / 10 / 12 / 16; in Clover's counting convention ~1.6 / 1.25 / 1.26 /
  1.41).
- **It is also the whole seldepth excess:** mean seldepth at depth 16 is
  30.3 with the extension and 25.1 without; Clover reports 25.8.
- It is not runaway chains: a ply cap at 1.5-2x root depth changes nothing.
  It is frequency: half of all eligible nodes extend, and the extended
  verification-borderline cases (within 30 cp of singular beta) are most of
  the cost (`sxm_30` keeps 92% of the saving).
- Everything else is small: check extension 10-13%, ALL-node reduction
  ~10-19%, the rest <= 5% each.

## 5. Quality: what the extra nodes buy

Same binary, variant vs main behaviour, 800 games (792 counted),
UHO book, Hash 16, Elo from the variant's view (pentanomial, 95% CI):

| variant | nodes to d16 | 200k nodes/move | 10+0.1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| no positive singular extension (`sex_0`) | x0.571 | **-46.8** [-58.7, -35.0] | **-28.1** [-40.9, -15.4] |
| SE only if clearly singular (`sxm_30`) | x0.599 | **-17.1** [-29.9, -4.4] | - |
| singular margin 4*d (`sem_4`) | x0.747 | -5.7 [-19.1, +7.6] | - |
| no check extension (`ce_0`) | x0.872 | +1.8 [-11.4, +14.9] | - |
| +1 LMR ply at expected ALL nodes (`allr_1`) | x0.814 | +10.1 [-3.2, +23.4] | - |

Fixed-node games already credit a variant for the depth its saved nodes
buy, so a variant that saves nodes and still loses at fixed nodes loses
value, not just time.

- **The singular extension is worth ~47 Elo per node.** Its node cost is
  real (x1.75 at depth 16) but buys more than the same nodes spent on
  depth. It is not artifacts either: the gap between singular beta and the
  verification score is spread out (of 86.7k tries at depth 13: 54% not
  singular, then gap 1 cp 3.9%, 2-10 10.2%, 11-30 8.6%, 31-100 10.6%,
  >100 12.9%), and dropping even the borderline (<= 30 cp) extensions loses
  17 Elo. Desktop sweep of that threshold (depth 16, x nodes): 10 cp 0.668,
  20 cp 0.627, 30 cp 0.599, 60 cp 0.599, 100 cp 0.582.
- So matching Clover's node count by cutting extensions would cost
  strength. Clover's smaller and (at equal depth it beat us by 51 Elo at depth 14 on 19 Nov) better
  tree must get the same selectivity some other way; from outside we can
  only see that its seldepth matches ours *without* the extension.
- The ALL-node reduction and the SE margin are the two levers that save
  nodes at no measurable per-node cost; both savings grow with depth, so
  their time value should be larger at LTC than in these fixed-node games.

## 6. Decomposition of the depth-12 gap (2.44x counted)

Multiplicative factors (counted nodes, rustfish / Clover):

| factor | depth 12 | depth 16 | depth 20 | evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| total (counted) | 2.44 | 3.06 | 3.80 | black box, 350 positions |
| node counting (frontier counted twice) | 1.26 | 1.24 | ~1.23 (extrapolated) | treestats 20.9% / 19.2%; black-box depth-1 counts |
| positive singular extension | 1.53 | 1.75 | 1.63 | `sex_0` x0.654 / x0.571 / x0.614 |
| check extension (with SE off) | 1.07 | 1.09 | 1.12 | `sex_0:ce_0` / `sex_0` |
| one ply less LMR at ALL nodes than the variant that holds value | 1.11 | 1.23 | 1.29 | `allr_1` x0.902 / x0.814 / x0.776 |
| unexplained remainder | ~1.07 | ~1.05 | ~1.17 | product of the rows above |

(Factors are measured one at a time; they interact a little.) Not
factors: extension chains (ply cap at 1.5-2x root depth: x1.00), aspiration
(window 20: x0.95-0.98; 12.7% of nodes are failed root searches, but a
narrower window is cheaper, not dearer), LMP/RFP/futility depth limits,
NMP aggressiveness, ProbCut, qsearch pruning, capture LMR, history pruning
(each x0.95-1.00). Turning IIR off costs x2.4: IIR is doing its job.

## 7. Proposals (one variable each, for LTC)

Each is one variable; the coordinator decides queueing (search changes go
`:ltc`). Ranked by expected value.

1. **P1 `tree-lmr-all-node`** (pushed, 03946db on main 3ca9a71, `Bench:
   170305`, perft/selftest/cargo test pass): late quiets at expected ALL
   nodes reduce one ply more (`r += 1` when `!pv_node && !cut_node`).
   Nodes to depth x0.90 / x0.81 / x0.78 at depth 12 / 16 / 20; +10.1
   [-3.2, +23.4] at 200k nodes. The saving grows with depth, so LTC is
   the right test. Follow-up if it passes: the same knob at 2 (`allr_2`,
   not measured) or scaled by depth.
2. **P2 `tree-se-margin-4`** (pushed, b3f6eb4 on main 3ca9a71, `Bench:
   184664`, tests pass): singular beta = TT score - 4*depth (was 2*depth).
   Nodes x0.88 / x0.83 / x0.75 at depth 10 / 12 / 16; -5.7 [-19.1, +7.6]
   at 200k nodes (even, within noise). A time-for-depth trade that only
   an LTC can judge; 3*depth (x0.82 at 16) is the conservative step if
   4 fails. (An earlier oracle screen of 3*depth was "only as good as
   searching 11% less"; never game-tested.)
3. **P3 aspiration window 20** (main 35): nodes x0.95-0.98, not game-tested;
   cheap LTC filler.
4. **P4 check extension restricted** (not built): removing it is neutral
   at fixed nodes (+1.8) with x0.87-0.84 nodes; a middle rule (extend only
   checks that do not lose material by SEE, or only at PV/cut nodes)
   could keep the tactics and part of the saving. Needs its own node +
   fixed-node screen first.
5. **P5 measurement hygiene (no Elo):** report peer comparisons in
   equal units. Either count a node once at the negamax-to-qsearch
   hand-off (changes `go nodes`, the bench number, datagen `softnodes`
   and every fixed-node SPRT; do it only deliberately), or keep the count
   and correct: the 200k-node Clover gauge gives us ~20% less work than
   Clover; quote ~-155..-160 instead of -178, or run the gauge at
   `nodes=250000` for rustfish vs 200000 for Clover (fastchess per-engine
   `nodes=`).
6. **Do not** cut the singular extension to save nodes (-47 / -28) or
   extend only clear singulars (-17).

## Reproduce

```bash
# black box + counters (GitHub runners, this branch's peer-match.yml)
gh workflow run peer-match.yml --ref diag-tree-gap -f rustfish_ref=diag-tree-gap -f limit=tree:bb:20 -f shards=8 -f games=16
gh workflow run peer-match.yml --ref diag-tree-gap -f rustfish_ref=diag-tree-gap -f limit=tree:kn:16:base/sex_0/sxm_30 -f shards=8 -f games=16
gh workflow run peer-match.yml --ref diag-tree-gap -f rustfish_ref=diag-tree-gap -f limit=tree:cnt -f shards=1 -f games=2
# knob games (variant vs main behaviour, same binary)
gh workflow run peer-match.yml --ref diag-tree-gap -f rustfish_ref=diag-tree-gap \
  -f peers_file=scripts/diag/treegap/peers.json -f opponent=rf-sxm_30 -f limit=nodes=200000 -f games=800
python3 scripts/diag/treegap/analyze.py bb <dir>; analyze.py stats '<dir>/*/rf-ts.jsonl'; analyze.py kn <dir>
```
(peer-match reports the main-behaviour build's view; the variant's Elo is
the negative.)
